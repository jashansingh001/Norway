## About Norway

Norway, a Scandinavian country, is known for its stunning natural landscapes, including fjords, mountains, and coastal regions. It boasts a rich history and a vibrant culture influenced by its Viking heritage. The capital city, Oslo, is a hub of art and history, featuring museums, galleries, and parks.

### Culture

Norwegian culture is unique and deeply rooted in traditions. The country celebrates various festivals, such as Constitution Day on May 17, which honors the signing of the Norwegian Constitution in 1814. Traditional foods, such as **rakfisk** and **klippfisk**, reflect the local cuisine, while folk music and dance illustrate the artistic side of Norway.

### Geography

From the northern lights in Tromsø to the midnight sun in Svalbard, Norway's geography offers breathtaking views. The fjords, such as Geirangerfjord and Nærøyfjord, are UNESCO World Heritage sites and attract many tourists each year. Hiking trails, skiing resorts, and picturesque villages are scattered throughout the country, inviting adventure seekers.

### Technology

Norway is at the forefront of technological innovation. The country invests in sustainable energy and advanced maritime technology, focusing on environmental preservation. Local startups are making strides in software development and digital solutions, contributing to Norway’s growth as a tech hub.
